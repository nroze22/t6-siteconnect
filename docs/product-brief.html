Executive Summary

TalOS SiteConnect is a desktop application that transforms how clinical research sites identify, screen, and enroll patients into clinical trials. By running entirely on-premise with zero cloud PHI exposure, SiteConnect eliminates the regulatory complexity of cloud-based screening tools while delivering AI-powered intelligence that was previously only available through enterprise SaaS platforms.

SiteConnect reduces manual chart review from hours to seconds, surfaces financial opportunities worth millions in aggregate, and provides Monte Carlo enrollment forecasting — all from a single desktop app that costs nothing to deploy.

The Problem

Clinical research sites face three interconnected challenges that reduce enrollment rates and leave revenue on the table:

  • Manual screening is slow and error-prone. Coordinators spend 4–8 hours per patient manually reviewing medical records against eligibility criteria. Across a typical site portfolio of 10–20 active studies, this creates a bottleneck that limits enrollment capacity.
  • Financial opportunity is opaque. Sites evaluate new studies based on per-patient payments without modeling total cost-of-research, screen failure risk, or net contribution. High-gross studies with heavy operational burden can actually lose money.
  • Population intelligence is nonexistent. Sites have no systematic way to understand what's in their patient population — which studies their patients match, what diversity gaps exist, or how enrollment rates would forecast for a given protocol.

The Solution

SiteConnect is a Tauri v2 desktop application (Rust backend + React 19 frontend) that provides:

Automated Screening

  • Two-tier evaluation: rule-based (Rust, sub-second) + LLM-assisted (BioMistral-7B, local)
  • 9 structured rule types covering all standard eligibility criteria
  • Evidence-linked results with confidence scoring
  • Clinician override with full audit trail
  • Automatic score-based patient ranking

Financial Modeling

  • 3-scenario modeling (Conservative / Base / Optimistic)
  • 20+ study archetypes with CMS fee schedule anchoring
  • Burden scoring across operational, startup, and screening dimensions
  • Break-even analysis and net contribution projections
  • One-click sponsor pitch PDF generation

Population Analytics

  • Feasibility queries with 6 criterion types and preset templates
  • Monte Carlo enrollment forecasting (1,000 simulations)
  • Simpson's diversity index with FDA census benchmarks
  • Lab trajectory analysis (patients approaching thresholds)
  • Auto-generated narrative insights

Research Intelligence

  • Curated research packs with 30+ real clinical trials
  • Sponsor profiles, pipeline trends, competition density
  • Adaptive study ranking by site profile preferences
  • Cohort builder with integrated forecasting
  • Multi-study patient matching for portfolio optimization

Architecture & Security

Core principle: No patient data ever leaves the device. SiteConnect runs 100% offline with an encrypted local database. There is no cloud component, no API calls with PHI, and no server-side processing of patient records.

Target Market

Primary: Independent Research Sites

Small-to-mid-sized clinical research sites (1–10 PIs, 2–20 coordinators) that lack enterprise technology budgets but need to compete for sponsor attention. These sites handle 5–30 active studies and screen 100–1,000+ patients per year.

Secondary: Academic Medical Center Research Programs

Department-level research programs within AMCs that need site-specific screening tools independent of their institution's central IT infrastructure, with strong emphasis on data governance.

Strategic: Site Network Building

SiteConnect serves as a "trojan horse" to build a site network for Talosix. As sites adopt the free screening tool, they become part of a data-informed network that can be leveraged for sponsor-facing services (feasibility, site selection, enrollment prediction).

Go-to-Market

  • Phase 1 — Free Distribution: SiteConnect is distributed as a free desktop installer. No account required. Sites download, install, import their data, and start screening immediately.
  • Phase 2 — Research Pack Subscriptions: Curated research packs (updated quarterly) provide financial benchmarks, sponsor profiles, and study intelligence. Free tier includes a starter pack; premium packs unlock industry-wide data.
  • Phase 3 — Network Services: Sites opt in to aggregate (de-identified) feasibility signals. Talosix leverages the site network for sponsor-facing services: feasibility assessments, site selection recommendations, enrollment predictions.
